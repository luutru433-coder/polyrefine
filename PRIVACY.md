# PolyRefine Translator Privacy Policy

PolyRefine Translator is a skill and a set of prompts that run inside ChatGPT. PolyRefine operates no backend or database, does not independently collect or store translation content, does not send it to external translation APIs, and does not require a third-party login. It does not transmit text outside ChatGPT.

OpenAI handles your ChatGPT conversations according to its [Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) and your plan, settings, and workspace policies. PolyRefine cannot disable ChatGPT Memory, history, retention, or training settings. See [Data Controls](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-faq) for the settings available to you.

For support or privacy questions, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/luutru433-coder/polyrefine/issues). Issues are public. Do not include translation content, private details, credentials, or customer data in an issue.
