# Soft Alternative (R02, R13)

After the main version, offer at most one useful softer English phrasing for negative content if it retains the original meaning. Do not add hedges such as “may”, “might”, “possibly” or “I believe” to reduce certainty; do not remove a material accusation, shift blame, change time or polarity, alter facts or add apologies or commitments. Keep the main version intact. If no genuinely useful softer wording preserves meaning, omit this section.
